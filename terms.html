Services

SPD Sports Therapy provides mobile sports massage, recovery and performance therapy, and injury prevention and maintenance services. The appropriate treatment is discussed with the client before or during a session.

Health and treatment

Sports therapy is not a substitute for diagnosis or treatment by a medical practitioner where medical care is required. Clients should disclose relevant injuries, conditions, medications and other information that may affect treatment, and should seek medical advice when appropriate.

Appointments

Appointments are arranged directly with SPD Sports Therapy. Please provide accurate contact details and let us know as soon as possible if you need to change an appointment. Any specific cancellation or rescheduling terms communicated when your appointment is arranged will apply.

Pricing and payment

Website prices are intended to show the current session rates supplied by SPD Sports Therapy. Confirm the applicable price when arranging your session. Payment arrangements are agreed when the appointment is booked.

Client responsibility

Clients are responsible for providing accurate information about their health, training and goals and for following reasonable aftercare guidance. Stop a treatment session and tell the therapist if you experience unexpected or significant discomfort.

Reviews

Reviews should reflect a genuine client experience. SPD Sports Therapy may decline to publish or use submissions that are abusive, misleading, unlawful or unrelated to the service.
