About

A mobile service with a practical focus.

SPD Sports Therapy is based in Durbanville and works with athletes and active people around Cape Town.

The idea

Good treatment should make sense to the person receiving it.

SPD Sports Therapy was built around a simple idea: recovery should not feel like a generic routine. Sessions are shaped around the person, their training and the areas that need attention.

The service focuses on sports massage, recovery and performance support, and ongoing maintenance. The aim is to help clients manage muscle tension, recover between training sessions and keep moving well.

Because the service is mobile, treatment can be arranged where it is convenient for you within the service area.

What matters
01

Personal

The session starts with your situation, not a preset script.

02
