Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

What we collect

If you contact SPD Sports Therapy by email, phone or WhatsApp, we may receive the details you choose to provide. If you submit a review, the form asks for your name, email address, sport or activity, rating and review text.

Why we use it

Information is used to respond to enquiries, arrange services, handle review submissions and communicate about the service. We do not need more information than is reasonably required for these purposes.

Review submissions

The review form is processed through Formspree. Information entered into that form is sent to the service and may be processed according to its own privacy practices. Please avoid putting sensitive personal information into a public review.

Third-party links

This website links to WhatsApp and Facebook. When you follow those links, the relevant third party's own privacy policy and terms apply.

Data choices

If you want to ask what information SPD Sports Therapy has received from you or request that a contact record be corrected, contact us at info.spdsportstherapy@gmail.com.
